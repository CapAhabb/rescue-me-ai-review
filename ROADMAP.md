# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Product Foundation

- Establish repository structure.
- Preserve Lake Command-family visual language for Rescue Me.
- Document architecture decisions.
- Keep local web and backend prototypes runnable.

## Phase 2: Incident Monitoring MVP

- Float plan creation and activation.
- GPS heartbeat ingestion.
- Route deviation detection.
- Emergency escalation policy engine.
- Emergency contact portal.

## Phase 3: Predictive Search

- Environmental provider registry.
- NOAA GLERL ERDDAP integration.
- Drift forecast model interface.
- Responder dashboard overlays.

## Phase 4: Platform Expansion

- Flutter mobile client.
- WebSocket mission updates.
- PostGIS-backed spatial data.
- Mission-specific modules for marine, outdoor, and disaster response.
